# RANSAC

> A sphere of **UD0** — the ROOT0 universe / biosphere. Domain: **A SCANNER DARKLY**.

RANSAC — A SCANNER DARKLY: a few wild outliers can drag a best-fit line completely off the truth, because least-squares trusts every point equally, even the liars. RANSAC does the opposite — guess a line from two random points, count how many agree, keep the guess with the most friends. It sees the real line THROUGH the noise. ⚑ LIVE full-house instrument: inliers on a line plus outliers, the RANSAC fit (robust, green) against the least-squares fit (dragged, red) parting ways as outliers rise; the two fits in 3D; tesseract 4D + shadow + toddler. ◆ LIT: score models by inlier count within a tolerance; a fail-loud self-check builds 40 points on y=3x+2 plus 25 wild outliers and throws unless RANSAC recovers slope ≈ 3 while least-squares is pulled &gt; 0.5 away. ▲ AMBER: randomised (seeded here), needs a sensible tolerance and enough iterations, fails past a majority of outliers. The scoring and its robustness are exact. Node-verified. AVAN, with David Lee Wise (ROOT0).
